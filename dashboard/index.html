How the value is worked out

Every implied value comes from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, seven-day growth, and how long the protocol has run with no token — never from a hand-kept list of names. The same function scores every row.

implied = TVL × fitted multiple × growth × maturity

  • Fitted multiple. A log-log regression of market cap on TVL across the snapshot's trading protocols (log mcap = intercept + slope × log TVL), fitted on the 104 trading protocols whose mcap/TVL sits between 0.02× and 5×, with a per-category effect from the median residual of that category's trading protocols. A category's effect is shrunk toward the global curve by its comparable count (shrinkage k = 9; a category with fewer than 3 comparables keeps only 10% of its effect, so a single comparable can no longer set a category's multiple). Each multiple is clamped to 0.02–2.00.
  • Growth. The seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, weighted at 0.5: a protocol growing 40% in a week scores 1.20×. A protocol that reports no seven-day change scores the snapshot's median change instead of zero.
  • Maturity. 0.80 for a brand-new protocol, rising to 1.00 after two years running with no token. A protocol with no listing date is scored as one year old.
  • Launch odds. The category's launch base rate — the share of the snapshot's protocols in that category that already trade — adjusted for age (older with no token, slightly less likely) and seven-day growth (growing, slightly more likely), clamped to 0.02–0.95. Expected value is implied value × odds; the ranking stays by implied value.
  • Uncertainty. Every row carries a p10–p90 implied-value range from the dispersion of the fit's log residuals (its own category's, when it has at least 5 fit protocols, else the global dispersion), and the rank range that range implies.
  • Farm verdict. The farmer's own net, not the protocol's value: the deposit is 0.02% of TVL (at least $100), the airdrop is assumed to be 7% of the token's value, and the farmer's share of it is deposit / TVL. Expected drop = implied × odds × 7% × (deposit / TVL); net = drop − the plan's gas; FARM when net is at least $25, MARGINAL when it is positive but below $25, SKIP when it is negative.

Out-of-sample evidence

Every term was added in turn and tested on rows it never saw, two ways: leave-one-out over the fit population, and walk-forward (fit on the oldest 60/70/80% of dated listings, test on the newer rest; the number shown is the median of the three splits' median errors). “× miss” is the median of max(predicted / actual, actual / predicted). The size curve is the big win; the other terms move the error by less than the split-to-split noise, and are kept because the task asks for them — the value follows from TVL, seven-day growth and how long it has run.

Category multiples

Derived from the snapshot's trading protocols: the fitted multiple at the category's median fit-population TVL (its pre-token median when it has no fit protocols). “n” is how many trading protocols the category effect rests on; a category with fewer than 3 keeps only 10% of its effect, so a single comparable cannot set the multiple. A fallback multiple — a category with no trading protocols at all — is the fitted curve with no category effect.
